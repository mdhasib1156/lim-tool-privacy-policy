Privacy Policy

LIM Tool — LAN IP Management Tool for Windows

Developed and published by HT Skills Hub

Last updated November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to LIM Tool, a Windows application developed and published by HT Skills Hub.

LIM Tool discovers and manages devices on your local network. The app does not require an HT Skills Hub account or send your device inventory, camera credentials, or live video to HT Skills Hub.

2. Information We Access and Process

  • Network discovery. The app reads network adapter and device information from Windows, such as IP addresses, MAC addresses, adapter names and gateway addresses. When you ask it to discover devices, it sends local discovery requests.
  • Device configuration. The app uses the addresses, ports and device credentials you enter to connect to selected devices, review settings and apply supported ONVIF IP changes when you request them.
  • Live view. When you start a live view, the app uses the camera address, stream settings and any supplied credentials to retrieve and display the stream. LIM Tool does not provide video recording or cloud storage.

3. Connections and Data Sharing

LIM Tool connects to the network devices you discover or select. It sends authentication and configuration requests to the target device when you use those features. To display the desktop interface and, where supported, relay live video to that interface, the app uses a service bound to your computer’s loopback address.

When you open a router’s settings from LIM Tool, your browser connects to the router. Information entered there is handled by the router and browser.

4. Local Storage and Removal

Device-type labels may be saved in local WebView storage. Information entered for configuration and live viewing is used during the active session. LIM Tool does not intentionally save camera passwords to a persistent app database. Windows and related components may manage their own caches or records.

Close LIM Tool to end the current session. To remove any locally retained labels, clear the app’s local WebView data where available.

5. Security and Your Choices

LIM Tool binds its interface service to the computer’s loopback address. Device authentication is sent to the selected device. If you choose an HTTP connection, it is not encrypted. Use HTTPS for device management where your device supports it, and use the app on networks and devices you are authorized to manage.

You can stop live viewing or close the app to end its active session. If you contact support, avoid sending passwords, camera footage, or other sensitive network information. You can email HT Skills Hub with questions about access to or removal of information you have shared with support.

6. Third-Party Components and Services

The Windows app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to display its interface. Compatible live viewing may use FFmpeg for video processing. Microsoft Store handles installation and updates for the Store version. This policy describes LIM Tool. Microsoft services and external websites handle information under their own policies.
